The solution

Five proven engines, one verified network.

1 · Proprio-direct
Sell without commission — fixed $1,199–$1,999 packages, AI pricing, notary on file (the DuProprio model, rebuilt)
2 · Marketplace
$450K condos to $40M sites, plus confidential business-for-sale listings under e-signed NDA
3 · Buy groups
Purchase together — pooled down payments, double-majority votes (C.c.Q. art. 1026), notarized agreements
4 · Capital platform
Invest from $100 — revenue-share LP units under NI 45-110, then OM & accredited raises via our own dealer
5 · Professional network
Licence-verified profiles (OACIQ, CNQ, RBQ…), subscriptions, discreet capped advertising
Clé IA
AI throughout — natural-language search, instant property assessments, seller pricing guidance (Claude API in production)
Each engine feeds the next: sellers → professionals → deal flow → investors3

Why now

Four doors opened at once.

  • The incumbent stalled. DuProprio: $45.6M revenue and NPS 61 (FY2017), then three ownership changes at a flat $50–60M — a one-product ceiling, not a small market
  • Co-ownership went institutional. Scotiabank, CMHC and Pacaso's US$72.5M round all bet on shared ownership in the last 36 months
  • The regulatory rails exist. Harmonized crowdfunding (2021), OM exemption, EMD path — mapped and modeled in our product already
  • Québec subsidizes the build. CDAEIA credits (20–30% of dev salaries), Impulsion PME matching, $200M Fonds Impulsion
Sources: Purplebricks RNS 2018; Desjardins 2020; BetaKit; Investissement Québec6

The moat

Compliance as architecture — hard to copy, harder to retrofit.

  • Verification ladder wired to professional registries (OACIQ, CNQ, OIQ, RBQ…) — trust as a data asset
  • Legal-lane router: every raise is born in the right exemption (45-110 / OM / accredited), with caps, escrow states and withdrawal timers enforced in code
  • Quebec-native: Civil-Code governance for buy groups, French-first product, notary integrations — structural friction for outside entrants
  • The flywheel compounds: every seller recruits professionals; every professional feeds deal flow; every deal seeds investors
